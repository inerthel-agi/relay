Features

Everything your chat shares, on screen.

Media to OBS

Images, GIFs from Tenor, Giphy and KLIPY, videos, audio and stickers play in order through one visual and one audio source.

Message notifications

Text posts become visual cards with the author's name and avatar, in OBS or in a desktop widget.

Manual moderation

Hold media for review and approve or reject each one from the panel. Nothing reaches the stream without your click.

Anti-doxxing scanner

A local scanner reads text in images and checks metadata to catch personal information before it is shown.

YouTube music

Viewers request songs from a dedicated channel. You reorder or remove them from the queue.

Sounds & reactions

Trigger short sounds with an image or GIF, with cooldowns and allowed roles you choose.

Desktop widgets

Transparent, always-on-top windows for media and notifications. Drag, lock, click through.

Panic button

One click clears every output and pauses new playback. Ctrl+Alt+S skips from anywhere.

Your look, your language

True-black theme, any accent color, adjustable font size, and an interface in 10 languages.

Privacy by design

It runs on your PC. It stays on your PC.

No account, no cloud service, no tracking. The bot only needs to read the channels you pick.

  • Local only. The output server listens on 127.0.0.1, never on your network.
  • Secrets protected. Your bot token is stored in Windows Credential Manager, not in a file.
  • Signed updates. Each release is verified with a pinned signature before it installs.
  • Open source. Read every line on GitHub, under the MIT license.

Control from Discord

Manage it without leaving your server.

Server administrators get a /relay command. Replies are only visible to them.

/relay channel #clips
/relay status outputs, queue, widgets
/relay test media · audio · notification
/relay reaction airhorn
/relay lock pause the channel
